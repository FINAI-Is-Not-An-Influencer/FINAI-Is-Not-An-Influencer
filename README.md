## 🚩 The Manifesto: Speculative Mysticism and Hacking the Self

I am a **Web Designer, Developer, and Marketer** operating at the intersection of **Pyrrhonian skepticism**, **digital ontology**, and the **hacking of the Self**.

I reject the definition of the web as a mere showcase for vanity. I interpret Web Development as a holistic, **ontological activity**: we do not just build pages; we structure collective human knowledge as a fundamental inquiry into the meaning of Being, rooted in a phenomenological and Heideggerian matrix.

My mission is to deconstruct the dogmatic architectures of digital reality. I maintain the structural rigor of dialectical materialism but reinterpret it through a mystical-magical lens: revolutionary praxis becomes a pure, constant practice of skeptical meditation. In this framework, Pyrrhonian skepticism is not an aporetic abyss, but rather a spiritually therapeutic remedy for the anxiety brought on by dogmatic desire. This path requires a Plotinian process of *aphairesis*—the deliberate removal of superficial cognitive additions to the soul—in order to achieve a true suspension of judgment (*epoché*) and ultimately lead the mind to a profound experience of Being.

I believe in **FLOSS (Free/Libre and Open Source Software)** as a prerequisite for this profound inner and outer liberation: **seizing the means of digital and spiritual production.**
